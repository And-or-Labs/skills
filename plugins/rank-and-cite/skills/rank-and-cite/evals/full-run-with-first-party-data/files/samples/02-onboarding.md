# The first ten minutes decide the next ten months

Activation is a boring word for a simple question: did the person get the thing they signed up for?

In our product, that thing is a first sent invoice. Accounts that send one inside a trial convert at roughly three times the rate of accounts that don't. That number has held for two years, which is longer than most of our assumptions have.

What moved it was not a tour. Tours are where good intentions go to be skipped. What moved it was deleting four fields from the signup form and pre-filling a sample client. Time to first invoice dropped from 26 minutes to 9.

If you want a rule: count the steps between signup and the first useful outcome, then remove one a week until someone complains.
